01

SANKALP

AI-powered UPSC preparation assistant · mobile app

UPSC aspirants juggle a huge syllabus with static timetables that can't adapt. Sankalp builds each day's plan from the student's real study history and keeps all study resources in one app.

  • Adaptive daily plan split into main subjects, revision and current affairs, with start, pause, complete, skip and move-to-tomorrow actions
  • Explainable analytics: progress per topic and subject, streaks, weak and strong topics, and automatic revision for topics not studied in 7+ days
  • Syllabus, marks scheme and PYQ links, plus 45 NCERT textbook links for Classes 6 to 12
  • Live current-affairs feed for India and International, filtered by category and time window
  • Secure backend with hashed passwords, signed tokens, rate limiting and automated tests

How the planner thinks

Each topic is scored from low accuracy, days since last studied, topics the student marked weak and preferred subjects. Missed tasks are kept in history and rank higher next time.

Honest status

Working end to end on a local setup. Next steps: online hosting, an installable Android build, and study reminders.

02

Dispatch Chatbot

Retrieval-based chatbot service · hosted on Render

A chatbot backend that answers questions and shows where the answer came from. It runs as its own online service, so other apps can use it through one chat endpoint.

  • Sourced answers: every reply returns its sources and retrieval time
  • Conversation memory through a conversation id
  • Disagreement flag when sources conflict, so users aren't misled
  • Plugged into Sankalp: the app attaches the student's real weak topics and today's plan only for personal questions. General questions are sent with no user data

Why it matters

Answers you can check are more trustworthy than answers you have to take on faith. Keeping personal data out of general questions also protects the user's privacy.
